## Open-source foundation

The Official Rules allow participants to include open-source software/hardware if licenses are followed and the submission enhances or builds upon it. OpenClaw's MIT license permits modification/distribution with its notice preserved. The foundation is permitted; eligibility is not established merely by forking or changing branding.

The guide requires the demo to identify and highlight event-created functionality. It expressly allows startups to extend existing products while identifying new work. That startup exception should not be treated as blanket permission to present the old Creative Claw product as new indie work. For an indie entry, define and demonstrate a newly built contribution using the disclosed open-source foundation.

## Product and submission
